Elevate your relaxation with the Aeris Duo 4D—a next-generation massage experience designed to move, adapt, and respond like human hands. Its advanced 4D roller technology combines with dynamic 3D depth control to deliver fluid, multidimensional movements while intelligently adjusting speed, rhythm, depth, and intensity in real time. From gentle relaxation to deep, targeted relief, the Aeris Duo creates a fully customizable massage that feels remarkably natural, immersive, and refreshingly personal.
Advanced
4D Dual Massage
The Aeris Duo’s 4D dual massage mechanism utilizes two independent sets of rollers working simultaneously—typically one targeting the neck and upper back while the second focuses on the lower back and glutes. By incorporating advanced 4D motion (moving up/down, side-to-side, in/out depth, and adjusting speed in real time), it simulates the feeling of two massage therapists.
Intelligent
Health Detection
The Aeris Duo 4D takes personalized relaxation to the next level with an advanced health monitoring system that tracks key metrics such as Health Rate, Microcirculation, Blood Oxygen, HRV, and Fatigue Index. Powered by AI-driven analysis, it evaluates your body’s real-time condition and intelligently recommends a massage program tailored to your needs—so every session feels uniquely suited to you.
360° Rotating
Footrest
The Aeris Duo 4D rotates a full 360° clockwise or counterclockwise, delivering a dynamic massage experience for your feet and lower legs. This fluid rotation helps ease stiffness, promote relaxation, and support healthy circulation for a more refreshed, rejuvenated feel.
Intelligent
Voice Control
Take control without lifting a finger. Intelligent Voice Control lets you adjust massage functions hands-free with simple voice commands, making it effortless to customize your massage and stay fully immersed in relaxation.
Detailed
Calf Massage
Experience a more immersive lower-leg massage with calf kneading massage, featuring dedicated rollers that work independently while integrated calf-rest bumpers move in sync with the kneading motion. Together, they create a dynamic, rhythmic massage that gently surrounds and targets the calves for deeper relaxation and relief.
Waist Cooling
Technology
Refresh and unwind with Waist Cooling Technology, delivering a gentle cool air to the waist for a soothing sensation during your massage. Paired with the chair’s heating therapy perfect for the colder months, it creates a balanced experience of cooling comfort and deep relaxation all year around.
Extra Foot
Massage Padding
Customize your comfort from the ground up with removable footrest padding, giving you the flexibility to add extra cushioning under your feet or remove it for a more personalized fit and massage experience.
Rocking
Mode
The Aeris Duo 4D features advanced Rocking Technology that gently sways the chair back and forth in a soothing, rhythmic motion. Designed to calm the body and ease tension, this cradle-like movement enhances relaxation and creates a deeply comforting massage experience.
SL-Track
The SL-Track System of Aeris Duo follows the natural contours of your body, extending from your neck and shoulders down through your back and glutes for smooth, continuous massage coverage and a more complete, immersive experience.
Body Scan
Advanced sensors scan your body’s shape, height, and pressure points to precisely map key areas like your neck, shoulders, and spine. The chair then automatically adjusts its rollers and massage settings for targeted comfort, deeper relief, and a truly personalized massage experience.
2-Stage
Zero Gravity Reclining
Inspired by technology originally pioneered by NASA, the Aeris Duo 4D features 2-Stage Zero Gravity that elevates your relaxation into a near-weightless experience. By positioning your body to help reduce pressure on the spine, it allows you to fully recline, unwind, and enjoy a deeper, more immersive massage from head to toe.
Space-Saving Technology
The Space-Saving Technology allows the chair to glide forward as it reclines, requiring minimal wall clearance while extending up to 2 inches for a full, comfortable recline without sacrificing valuable space.
44-Cell Air
Compression
Massage
Experience soothing full-body relief with the 44-Cell Air Compression Massage, which uses strategically placed air cells to gently compress and release key areas of the body. Designed to promote relaxation and enhance your massage experience, it delivers a comforting, rhythmic sensation from shoulders to feet.
Lumbar
Heating Therapy
Soothe tension and enhance relaxation with Heating Therapy, which delivers gentle, targeted warmth to the lower back to help loosen tight muscles and create a deeper, more comforting massage experience.
* The heating therapy provides warmth in the range of approximately 75 - 83°F.
